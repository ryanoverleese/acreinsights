Soil Temps

Soil temperature and soil water, by depth.

Live readings from Acre Insights soil probes, updated every hour.

Loading...

Readings come from Sentek soil probes with sensors at nine depths, from 2″ down to 33″. Each probe logs every 30 minutes, and this page picks up new readings every hour. Temperatures are in °F.

The probes don’t have a 4″ sensor, so the top number is the average of the 2″ and 6″ sensors.
